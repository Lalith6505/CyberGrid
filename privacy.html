Privacy Policy

Application: Cyber Grid: Infiltration Protocol
Developer: Lalith Madanayake
Effective Date: October 15, 2026
Key Summary: Cyber Grid: Infiltration Protocol is a 100% offline, privacy-first turn-based tactical matrix roguelike game. It does NOT collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal data, analytics, location, advertising IDs, or telemetry.

1. Information Collection and Use

Cyber Grid: Infiltration Protocol is designed from the ground up to respect player privacy:

  • Zero Personal Information: We do not ask for, collect, or store any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, location, or physical address.
  • Zero Telemetry & Analytics: The app does not include third-party analytics trackers, crash telemetry services, behavioral trackers, or user profiling SDKs.
  • Local-Only Game Storage: All game data, including sector progression, nano-chips earned, high scores, and audio/haptic preferences, is saved exclusively on your local device via encrypted Android SharedPreferences / local browser storage. This data never leaves your device and is erased upon app uninstallation.

2. Permissions Requested

The application requests only minimal, necessary system permissions to operate:

  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Exclusively used to deliver tactile haptic feedback during player actions (slashes, EMP pulses, and collisions). Can be disabled at any time in the in-game Settings menu.

3. Advertisements & Monetization

Cyber Grid: Infiltration Protocol is a strictly 100% ad-free premium application:

  • No banner ads, interstitial ads, rewarded video ads, or native ads.
  • Zero third-party advertising SDKs or tracking pixels.
  • Zero in-app purchases or subscription fees.

4. Children’s Privacy

Because Cyber Grid: Infiltration Protocol does not collect any personal data from any user regardless of age, it is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), GDPR-K, and Google Play Families Policy Requirements. Users of all ages can safely enjoy the game without any risk of data exposure.

5. Third-Party Services

This application does NOT incorporate any third-party SDKs, tracking frameworks, social media logins, or external API endpoints. The game runs entirely offline.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in legal or platform standards. Any updates will be published immediately on this page with a revised effective date.
